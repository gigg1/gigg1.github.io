<p>Hello there! I am <strong>Mutong LIU</strong> (刘牧潼), a Ph.D. candidate in the <strong><a href="https://www.comp.hkbu.edu.hk/v1/">Department of Computer Science at Hong Kong Baptist University</a></strong>, supervised by <strong><a href="https://www.comp.hkbu.edu.hk/v1/?page=profile&id=csygliu">Prof. Yang LIU</a></strong> and co-supervised by <strong><a href="https://www.comp.hkbu.edu.hk/v1/?page=profile&id=jiming">Prof. Jiming LIU</a></strong>. Prior to that, I received a Bachelor of Engineering from Southwest University (Chongqing, China).</p>
<p>My primary research interests include <strong>artificial intelligence, machine learning, reinforcement learning, computational epidemiology, and complex system modeling</strong>, specifically focusing on developing multi-agent reinforcement learning algorithms, physical/epidemiological-informed machine learning methods, and spatiotemporal prediction and analysis approaches, as well as their applications in practical scenarios.</p>
